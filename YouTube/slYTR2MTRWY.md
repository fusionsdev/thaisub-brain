# Thai Summary: FARMING GOOGLE ADS ACCOUNTS 2026

## Video Overview
This video explains the complete process of preparing Google Ads accounts for successful advertising campaigns in 2026. The speaker (PPA) demonstrates how to "farm" accounts to build trust with Google's systems and avoid account suspension.

## Key Topics Covered

### 1. Account Farming Fundamentals
- **What is account farming**: Process of building natural user behavior history
- **Why it's critical**: Google's anti-fraud systems are now much stricter
- **Main goals**: Create realistic behavioral signals, avoid suspicion, establish trust

### 2. Account Preparation Methods
- **Self-registration with manual farming**: 14-22 days, full control
- **Agency account rental**: Quick start but with fees and deposits
- **Accounts with spending history**: Most stable but expensive

### 3. 21-Day Farming Process
**Days 1-3: Basic Setup**
- Create Gmail with 2FA
- Perform Google searches (20-30 seconds each)
- Use YouTube (watch videos, subscribe, comment)
- Upload 2-3 files to Google Drive

**Days 4-7: Activity Expansion**
- Search locations on Google Maps
- Install apps from Google Play
- Login to third-party websites
- Use Google Calendar and Sheets

**Day 8: Google Ads Creation**
- Use geo-matching proxy IP
- Learn the system interface
- Pause account for 1 day

**Day 9: Credit Card Integration**
- Perform previous activities
- Link card to Google Pay
- Link same card to Google Ads after 1-2 hours

**Days 10-17: Trust Building**
- Add Google Partners (Skillshop)
- Study and use Google Ads regularly
- Multiple daily activities
- Show account engagement

**Days 18-19: Account Rest**
- No Google Ads activities
- Random Google ecosystem activities

**Day 20: Testing**
- Spend $10-20 on White Offer
- Convert account from "empty" to "alive"

**Day 21: Real Campaign Launch**
- Account ready for actual advertising

### 4. Critical Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Too fast actions**: Immediate card linking, no pre-warming
2. **No realistic behavior**: Only Google Ads activities, no Google ecosystem
3. **Service overload**: Too many activities in short time
4. **Ignoring rest period**: No account maturation time

### 5. Basic Principles
- Perform realistic, trustworthy activities
- Work slowly and patiently
- Create consistent usage history
- Ensure geo-data consistency
- Use geo-matching proxy IP
- Use same credit card across Google services
